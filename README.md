## What is 2k Music?

2k Music is a next-generation desktop music player that brings the power of YouTube Music to your desktop with a stunning, feature-rich interface. Built with modern web technologies and wrapped in a native desktop experience, it's the music player you've been waiting for.

**No subscriptions. No limits. Just music.**

## Features

### **Beautiful Interface**
- Modern, sleek design inspired by the best music players
- Smooth animations and transitions
- Dark mode optimized for late-night listening
- Responsive layout that adapts to your workflow

### **Powerful Playback**
- High-quality audio streaming
- Smart queue management with shuffle and repeat
- Seamless track transitions

### **Your Library, Your Way**
- Create and manage unlimited playlists
- Like and save your favorite tracks

- Powerful search across millions of tracks
- Artist biographies and images
- Album artwork and metadata

### **Offline Playback**
- Download tracks for offline listening
- Parallel downloads for faster speeds
- Automatic quality selection
- Manage your downloaded library

### **Advanced Features**
- System-wide equalizer integration (EqualizerAPO)
- Real-time lyrics display
- Keyboard shortcuts and media keys support
- Discord Rich Presence integration
- Notification system for track changes
- Mini player mode for multitasking

### **Privacy & Control**
- Your data stays on your device
- No tracking or analytics
- Secure authentication with Google
- Offline mode support

## Advanced Setup

### Equalizer Integration

2k Music supports system-wide audio enhancement through EqualizerAPO:

1. The installer will prompt you to install EqualizerAPO
2. Follow the setup wizard to configure your audio device
3. Restart your computer after installation
4. Access equalizer presets in Settings → Audio

### Custom Domain Setup

For optimal YouTube playback, the installer configures a custom domain (`2kmusic.local`):
- Automatically added to your hosts file during installation
- Enables unrestricted YouTube embed playback
- Completely safe and reversible
- Removed automatically on uninstall

## Troubleshooting

**App won't start?**
- Make sure you're running Windows 10 or later
- Try running as Administrator
- Check if antivirus is blocking the app

**No sound?**
- Check your system volume
- Verify audio device in Settings
- Restart the app

**Download issues?**
- Check your internet connection
- Verify you have enough disk space
- Try downloading one track at a time

**Equalizer not working?**
- Ensure EqualizerAPO is installed
- Restart your computer after installation
- Check that your audio device is configured
